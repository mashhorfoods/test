## 2. The decisions being frozen

Approving this gate freezes these. Changing one afterwards is a change request,
not a revision — that is the point of a gate.

1. **Market and model.** A remote studio serving the Gulf and Egypt. No office,
   no local presence claimed.
2. **Positioning.** One partner instead of four; remote by design; a price a
   growing business can start at.
3. **Prices and currency.** The USD ladder in `src/data/pricing.json` — at or
   just under the Egyptian floor, clearly under the Gulf band.
4. **Primary conversion.** A package-aware WhatsApp message. Form second, phone
   and email visible, `mailto:` as a fallback that cannot fail silently.
5. **The KPI set**, and that nothing is judged until analytics has 30 days.
6. **Information architecture.** Five public pages; `/work` opens at two
   anonymised case studies; one URL serves both languages until Arabic demand
   is measured.
7. **Proof strategy.** Anonymised client work, a named human, a kept response
   window, third-party listings — no invented numbers, ever.
8. **No CMS, no backend, no dashboard**, with the triggers in
   `docs/36` §4 as the only route back.
9. **The seven wireframes** as the structure for what gets built next.
10. **What is not being built:** chat widget, empty testimonial slots, blog
    programme, form wizard, popups, per-country pages, e-commerce.
