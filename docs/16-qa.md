# Stage 16 — Global QA & Production Readiness

Not a redesign. An audit of the finished homepage, the fixes it turned up, and
an honest list of what it could not fix.

The audit is `scratchpad/audit16.js`: ten groups, run against the whole page in
both directions. Everything below is measured, not eyeballed.

---

## 1. What was broken, and is now fixed

### Two sequence numerals failed contrast at 1.86:1

`.c-pillar__index` (Why Us) and `.c-step__num` (Process) were set in
`--color-border-strong` — 20% white, measuring **1.86:1** against their ground.
The large-text floor is 3:1.

Both are `aria-hidden`, and it would have been easy to call them decoration and
move on. They are not: they are the **visual order cue**, and a low-vision
reader needs them exactly as much as anyone else. Both now use
`--color-text-muted` and measure **4.98:1** — clearing even the 4.5:1
small-text floor, while still reading a full step quieter than the white name
beside them.

### The page shipped Stage 01 scaffold metadata

```html
<title>Digital Agency — Homepage</title>
<meta name="description" content="Homepage shell. Header and navigation are
complete; page sections are built in later stages." />
```

That was the shipped `<title>` and search-result snippet of a finished
sixteen-stage site. It now names the agency and describes the actual offering,
with `og:site_name`, `og:description`, `og:locale` (`en_US` + `ar_SA`
alternate) and `twitter:card` added.

### Three dead components removed

Zero references anywhere in the markup, the styleguide or the scripts:

| Component | Why it existed, and why it does not |
| --- | --- |
| `.c-reveal` | A hover-disclosure. Every section that needed supplementary detail ended up rendering it at rest instead, which is the better answer. |
| `.c-rule` | An accent divider flourish. Every section draws its own rule with a border, which keeps it attached to what it divides. |
| `.c-header-spacer` | A page offset from when the header was going to be `fixed`. It ended up `sticky`, which reserves its own space. |

### Two more, found by the same sweep and fixed in Stage 15

The navigation-system QA (§13/§33) is what surfaced them, so they belong on
this list: **the page had no navigation at all with JavaScript disabled**, and
the footer said *Digital Marketing & Ads* where the Services section says
*Digital Marketing & Advertising*. Both are recorded in `docs/15-footer.md`.

## 3. Three findings deliberately not "fixed"

**The oversized footer wordmark sits at 1.86:1.** It is `aria-hidden`, and its
text is already present, linked and at full contrast in the brand block
directly above it. WCAG 1.4.3 exempts pure decoration, and raising it would
make a closure device shout. This is the audit's **one** contrast exemption and
it is named explicitly in the test rather than absorbed into a general rule
that happens to let it through.

**Thirty-five design-system classes are defined and never used** — `l-*` layout
primitives, `u-*` utilities, `t-small`, `t-measure-tight`, and three `c-card`
sub-parts. These are Stage 00's documented API, specified before they were
needed on purpose. Removing them would break `docs/00-design-system.md` and
would be a redesign of a completed stage. They cost about 9KB uncompressed and
they stay.

**Three animations loop** — two hero orbit bodies at 26s and 38s, and the
scroll cue at 2s. All are Stage 02 ambience, all stop under
`prefers-reduced-motion`, which is the property that actually matters. The
audit asserts that, rather than asserting the animations do not exist.

## 4. What the audit could not fix

**`#contact` is still a placeholder**, and it is the destination of every CTA
on the site. There is no approved email address, phone number, WhatsApp number
or form endpoint anywhere in the supplied data, so there is nothing to build
it from. It is the last piece of placeholder content on the page and the only
thing standing between this site and a working conversion.

`og:url`, the canonical, `og:image` (1200×630), `apple-touch-icon.png`
(180×180) and Organization JSON-LD are all still TODO for the same reason: they
need a domain, raster exports of the logo, and the legal entity details.

## 5. The checklist

Every line of §34 is covered by the audit or by a stage suite, except:

- **No placeholder content** — fails on `#contact`, for the reason above.
- **Browser compatibility is verified** — verified in headless Chromium only.
  The stack is plain semantic HTML, `@layer`, logical properties, `:has()`,
  container queries and CSS grid; nothing is prefixed and nothing is
  Chromium-specific, but Firefox and Safari have not been run here. Worth a
  pass on real devices before launch.

Everything else passes, measured.
