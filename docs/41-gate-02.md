## 3. Component rules being frozen

From `docs/38-master-prompt.md` §6, now demonstrated in built code:

1. **One primary action per view.** On package cards only the featured tier is
   primary — three yellow buttons per service is how the page once reached 83.
2. **Every CTA carries its context** — service, package, price, billing, in the
   reader's language, generated from the same file as the card.
3. **Cards are objects, not decoration.** Border, fill and radius mean
   "separate thing"; the verification band is a band, not a card.
4. **Prices are typeset, not shouted** — figure, currency, billing, in that
   order, currency and billing quieter than the number.
5. **Proof is linked, never asserted.** A proof slot with no live destination is
   omitted rather than greyed.
6. **Scope facts sit under a hairline**, three visible, the qualifying detail
   behind one native disclosure.
7. **The header holds five items.** Anything else lives in the drawer and the
   footer.

Templates: T1 long-form sales page · T2 narrative page · T3 content page
(About, Pricing, Privacy). A fourth content page costs nothing.

## 5. The risk this gate asks you to accept

**No moderated sessions with real buyers were run.** Phase 12 walked every
journey and proved the site does what it claims in every state — but a logic
review cannot prove comprehension. Nobody outside this project has been watched
using it.

Unknown, and only findable that way: whether the price bands read as reasonable
to a Gulf or Egyptian buyer · whether "Ask about Starter" feels like a
commitment or an invitation · whether the Arabic reads naturally to someone who
speaks it · whether anyone notices the case study has no result.

**Approving Gate 02 means accepting that risk knowingly.** The script is ready
in `docs/33-wireframes.md` §9 — five people, five tasks, about two hours — and
it can still be run after the gate; findings would then arrive as change
requests rather than as design work.
